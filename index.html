    What the lab does

    Eight instruments, one laboratory.

    AI molecule generator

    Turn a therapeutic goal into candidate structures with predicted properties.

    Virtual synthesis lab

    Run reagents through a flask and see how heat, concentration and catalysts change yield.

    Drug interaction simulator

    Dock compounds into a target pocket and watch occupancy build and fall.

    Pharmacology engine

    Follow absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion through the body.

    Disease target explorer

    Compare on-target and off-target binding to see where side effects come from.

    Autonomous research agents

    Agents design runs, screen compounds, analyse results and refine designs.

    Compound marketplace

    Publish, share and license molecular models, datasets and experiment setups.

    Discovery leaderboard

    Compete on predicted affinity, selectivity, drug-likeness and reproducibility.
